---
name: rules-defer
description: When a review finding is patched, deferred or dismissed — patch by default; defer only for scope outside the story or a design or spec decision, named and tracked; never for effort, size or "bar impact none". Preloaded into the review layers and findings-evaluator; read at parallel-dev-wave triage.
user-invocable: false
---

- **Patch by default.** A real finding whose fix lies within this story is a patch, whatever its size. A cheap fix is a patch.
- **Defer only when the fix needs scope outside this story or a design or spec decision.** Scope outside this story: a change to code, a contract or a document that this story neither changes nor covers in an AC. A decision: a design, UX or spec call this story's spec does not make.
- **A harness fix is outside a product story's scope.** A fix to the dev-lifecycle harness (`.workflow/`, `.claude/`, `.opencode/`, hook and settings files) that the story's ACs do not cover is deferred to an Open row of `config: paths.harness_backlog` and carved as its own story, even when this story's review surfaced it and even when it blocks this story. The product close-out holds product patches only.
- **A defer names that scope or decision and where it is tracked.** The tracking row names the finding as `<story_id> <finding_id>` in one of:
  - the epic block (`config: paths.epics_dir`) of a story not yet done whose scope covers the fix;
  - a new story's epic block and sprint-status row (a carve: the owner's call);
  - an Open row of `config: paths.harness_backlog` (harness scope no story covers; a pending decision reads "owner rules first" in its Next cell);
  - an Open row of `config: paths.product_backlog` (product scope no not-done story covers; its Next cell reads "owner carves");
  - an open question in the epic block of the story that owns the decision (a spec, design or UX decision).

  The story's own `<config: paths.deferred_work_dir>/story-<id>.md` entry is the log, not the tracking row.
- **Never a defer reason:** effort, size, low severity, a narrow trigger, a test-only gap, `bar impact: none`. The loop exit, the no-behaviour batch, the round 3 stop and the auto-mode rule below are the exceptions.
- **Loop exit.** From review round 2 on, a round's delta is the change `review_stage.py` stages for a close-out round: the close-out patch against the close-out the latest triaged round reviewed (the round's Summary `Delta:` line). The renderer marks each finding `inside` or `outside` it, and the round `Loop exit: candidate` when every finding lies outside it and none is bar-blocking. A candidate round with no never-deferred finding ends the story's review loop: each of its findings is deferred, named and tracked as above, and no further round runs. A never-deferred finding anywhere in the round is patched and the loop continues.
- **No-behaviour batch.** From review round 2 on, a finding whose fix changes no behaviour (a code comment, a doc or bookkeeping line, or a missing test where the code is already correct) and whose bar impact is `none` or `n/a` is not patched in the close-out. All such findings of the round go into one tracking row naming each `<story_id> <finding_id>`: the epic block of a not-done story covering the code, a product-backlog Open row, or a harness-backlog Open row for harness code. A change to agent, skill or rule instructions is behaviour, not batched. A never-deferred finding is never batched.
- **Round 3 stop.** From review round 3 on, only never-deferred findings are patched. Every other confirmed finding of the round goes into the round's one tracking row, as in the no-behaviour batch.
- **Round cap.** In both modes the review loop ends after the second close-out round that follows the branch review (round 3 in the normal case). Its never-deferred findings are patched and verified by the scoped tests, and the story goes to the pre-merge gate and merge with no further review round, except the one New code after the last round below.
- **Last round's patches.** In a round no further review round follows (the round cap, a skipped round, or a round below the round-2 trigger), every approved patch that changes code carries a regression test that fails without the patch: its `repro_test`, or one `story-finalize` writes. A patch to skill, agent, rule, spec or doc text needs none.
- **New code after the last round** (controlled mode). A patch or ruling of the last round that adds code beyond the fix of its finding (a new module, function, hook or component, or a redesign of existing code) gets one of two outcomes, the owner's call: one review round of that change alone after story-finalize amends it in (`review_stage.py --new-code-since <close-out before the change>`), whose never-deferred findings are patched under Last round's patches with no further round; or a carve of the change as its own story, the finding deferred to it. A finding's own fix, however large, is not new code.
- **Never deferred:** a defect this story's change or fix introduces (a regression, or a bug in code the story adds), a deviation from this story's ACs, a security gap, a bar-blocking finding (`rules-bar-impact`). Each is patched. A fix that reaches code outside this story's changed lines is still a patch, with the recommended fix. It goes to human triage instead (the owner at parallel-dev-wave triage; findings-evaluator's `## Needs human review`) only when the fix needs a feature or scope the spec does not contain, contradicts the spec's ACs or a source-of-truth doc, or when the AC itself is wrong.
- **Auto mode (auto-dev-loop).** The round 3 stop applies from the first close-out round (round 2 in the normal case); a Loop exit round's findings also go into the round's one tracking row. `review_ledger.review_loop` (`review_stage.py`) counts the rounds and refuses a round the loop does not owe.
- **Dismiss only** a false positive, a duplicate or redundant finding, a finding another patch already fixes, or one that does not apply to the project's stage (the quality-bar gate runs first).
- **A review layer's `defer`** names, in its `claim`, the scope or decision the fix waits on; the triager names the tracking row.
